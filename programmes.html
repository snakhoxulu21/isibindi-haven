Daily Soup Kitchen

Every day of the week, our soup kitchen serves warm, nutritious meals to anyone in need. Run entirely by volunteers, the kitchen has become a trusted, welcoming space in the heart of Durban's inner city.

Mobile Health Clinic

Once a week, a team of volunteer nurses and doctors brings free basic healthcare directly to areas where it's needed most, including check-ups, wound care, and referrals to further medical support.

Second Chance Programme

Our flagship programme equips participants with practical, job-ready skills — from CV writing and interview preparation to short skills courses — helping many re-enter the workforce and rebuild independent lives.
