Every person deserves dignity, care, and a second chance.

Isibindi Haven supports Durban's homeless and vulnerable community members through daily meals, basic healthcare, and practical job-readiness training.

Who We Are

Founded in 2016, Isibindi Haven began as a small group of former social workers and volunteers operating out of a converted church hall in Durban's inner city. Today we run a daily soup kitchen, a weekly mobile health clinic, and our "Second Chance" job-readiness programme.
