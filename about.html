Our History

Isibindi Haven was founded in 2016 by a small group of former social workers and community volunteers who saw a growing need for coordinated, compassionate support for Durban's homeless population. What began as a single soup kitchen operating out of a converted church hall has since grown into a multi-programme organisation reaching hundreds of people every month.

Our Mission

To restore dignity to Durban's homeless and vulnerable individuals through access to meals, basic healthcare, and practical skills training that leads to sustainable employment.

Our Vision

A Durban where every individual, regardless of circumstance, has access to the care, support, and opportunity needed to rebuild their life.
